# Production QA checklist

This checklist is evidence for human review, not a replacement for the target platform's current preflight or a physical proof.

## Source and structure

- [ ] Publication identity, rights, and research trail are complete
- [ ] Scene plan and page map agree
- [ ] Page count is final and recorded
- [ ] Trim, bleed, reading direction, and page sides are intentional
- [ ] Blank reverse pages and marker bleed-through are considered
- [ ] Every scene has an accessible description or equivalent text

## Art and assets

- [ ] Every asset has a source, creator, rights, and review state
- [ ] Candidate, keeper, print, and released states are not conflated
- [ ] Print assets have genuine detail at the intended placed size
- [ ] Effective resolution is at least the target profile's minimum
- [ ] Line weight and safe areas are checked at final size
- [ ] No accidental grayscale, generation debris, crop marks, or placeholder text remains

## Target and proof

- [ ] Current target specification and template were rechecked
- [ ] Interior PDF and cover wrap were assembled from the recorded edition
- [ ] Platform preview/preflight passed
- [ ] Physical proof was ordered only with explicit human approval
- [ ] Physical proof was reviewed and corrections were resolved
- [ ] Final release metadata and identifiers are real, not placeholders
