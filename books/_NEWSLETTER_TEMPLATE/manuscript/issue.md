# Newsletter Title — Issue 1

## From the editor

Start with the reason this issue is worth opening now. Keep it brief enough that the reader reaches the substance quickly.

## Briefs

Use short paragraphs for updates, links, releases, events, or other items that do not need a full feature.

## Feature

Give the main piece room to breathe. A newsletter can still use figures, citations, footnotes, and ordinary Markdown links when the material calls for them.

## Recommended reading

Collect a small number of external sources with enough context that the reader knows why each link belongs here.

## Housekeeping

Put subscription notes, schedule changes, corrections, acknowledgments, and contact information here rather than scattering them through the issue.
