# Your Newspaper — Edition Date

Lead with the story readers should see first. A Bookself newspaper can be a neighborhood weekly, campus paper, trade newspaper, community bulletin, or another edition-based publication; the source is still plain Markdown.

## Front page

Write the lead story here, with the strongest verified facts near the top. Add images from `../media/` when they belong with the reporting.

## Around the desk

Use the rest of the edition for the sections this newspaper actually needs: local reporting, business, culture, sports, opinion, notices, data, corrections, or something more specialized. Do not keep a section merely because a traditional newspaper has one.

## Corrections and notes

Record material corrections clearly. Git history preserves earlier versions, while this section gives ordinary readers a visible account of changes that matter to the published edition.
