# Paper Title

## Abstract

State the question, the approach, the main result, and why it matters in a few clear paragraphs.

## Introduction

Give readers the context they need and define the problem precisely.

## Methods

Describe the method, data, experiment, model, or process well enough that another person can understand what was done.

Figures live in `media/` and use ordinary Markdown:

`![Concise description of the figure](../media/figure-1.png)`

## Results

Report the result before interpreting it. GitHub-flavored Markdown tables work here as well.

## Discussion

Explain what the result means, what it does not mean, and the limitations that matter.

## References

Use the citation style appropriate to your field. Keep persistent links and DOIs when they are available.
