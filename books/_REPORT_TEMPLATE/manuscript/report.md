# Report Title

## Executive summary

State the problem, the most important findings, and the decisions or consequences that follow. Keep claims proportional to the evidence in the body of the report.

## Scope and method

Explain what was examined, the time period, sources, definitions, assumptions, and any important limitations.

## Findings

Present the evidence in a sequence a reader can audit. Use figures, tables, citations, and footnotes where they add information rather than decoration.

## Discussion

Interpret the findings, including uncertainty, competing explanations, and what the evidence does not establish.

## Recommendations or next actions

Include this section only when the report is meant to recommend action. Tie each recommendation to a finding rather than introducing new claims here.

## Appendix

Put supplementary methods, definitions, data notes, disclosures, and other material here when it would interrupt the main report.
