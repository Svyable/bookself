# Back Matter

Use only the sections this book needs. Delete the rest rather than filling space
because a template offered it.

## Epilogue

Optional. End the argument or story where the reader should leave it.

## Notes

Optional. Use for explanatory or source notes that belong together at the back
rather than interrupting the reading flow.

## References

Optional. Give readers the sources they should be able to identify and recover.
A bibliography, works cited, or sources heading is equally valid when that fits
the book better.

## Further Reading

Optional. Point readers toward useful next steps without pretending every useful
book or article was a direct source.

## Glossary

Optional. Define terms whose meaning matters across the work.

## Appendices

Optional. Put supporting material here when it is useful but would overload the
main narrative.

## Index

Optional. Index concepts, people, places, cases, works, symbols, or other things
readers may reasonably want to find again. In a reflowable digital edition,
prefer durable chapter or section destinations over temporary Reader page
numbers. Fixed print or PDF editions may use their own edition-specific page
locators.

## Acknowledgments

Optional. Thank the people and institutions that materially helped the work.

## About the Author

Optional. A short paragraph about who wrote the book.
