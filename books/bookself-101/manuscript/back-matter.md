# Back Matter

## Instructor note

Bookself 101 is deliberately small. It demonstrates a course-text pattern rather than prescribing a pedagogy. An instructor can keep the same publication shape and replace the subject matter completely.

A useful academic Bookself publication might include chapter readings, figures in the publication's `media/` folder, references as ordinary Markdown links, mathematical notation where the subject requires it, and exercises or discussion prompts at the end of a chapter. Teaching assistants can propose corrections in reviewable branches. Students can read the released edition without needing to understand Git.

## A semester rhythm

One practical rhythm is to keep the assigned edition on the public Shelf for the duration of the course while the private Binder collects corrections, new examples, and next-semester revisions. At an intentional release point, the instructor can review the new edition as a whole and replace the Shelf snapshot.

Git history then serves as more than a backup. It records the editorial history of the teaching material: what changed, when it changed, and the explanation attached to the change.

## About this example

This book is part of the Bookself platform demonstration. It uses the same Markdown publication format and Reader as any other Bookself book. The academic conventions in its chapters are examples, not special platform features.
