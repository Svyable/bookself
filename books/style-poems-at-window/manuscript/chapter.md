# Four small windows

## Morning glass

The window keeps the cold outside  
but not the color of it.  
Blue enters first,  
then the shape of the chair.

A kettle begins  
its small weather.

## Commuter

Across the street  
a person ties one shoe  
while standing,  
late enough to balance  
on one ordinary miracle.

The bus arrives  
without metaphor.

## Noon

Nothing dramatic:  
a square of sun  
moves from the floor  
to the lower shelf.

Dust becomes visible  
only when illuminated,  
which seems unfair.

## After rain

Each parked car  
wears a second sky.

A child steps directly  
into the deepest reflection  
and breaks it  
with both feet.
