# Reading to return

Study reading is different from reading only for momentum. The reader expects to stop, compare, reread, make a note, follow a reference, and return to the same passage later.

The `quiet-study` preset is built around that slower pattern. It recommends a narrow measure, generous leading, left alignment, and a calm sage atmosphere. The type remains literary enough for long passages but the page avoids the stronger conventions of novel typography.

## What the layout is trying to support

A study page should make it easy to:

- find the beginning of the next line;
- distinguish headings at a glance;
- pause without losing your place;
- scan a list or quotation;
- return to a paragraph after looking away.

Continuous Scroll mode keeps nearby sections in spatial relation, which can help when moving between explanation and examples.

### A small experiment

Read this paragraph once at the recommended width. Then open the Reading experience panel and switch to a wide measure. Neither setting is universally correct. The useful question is which one makes *this* reading task easier for *you*.

That is why the preset is a starting condition rather than an enforced classroom style.

A professor can recommend the environment in which a course text was designed. A student can still change the typeface, size, contrast, alignment, spacing, or mode in their own browser without changing the published work for anyone else.
