# Bookself All Rights Reserved profile v1

Identifier: `bookself-arr-v1`

This is Bookself's conservative default rights profile for an author-owned publication. It is a policy profile, not a substitute for the publication's own copyright notice or `RIGHTS.md`.

The profile means:

- ordinary reading of an official public copy is permitted;
- conventional search discovery is permitted;
- no additional license is granted for reproduction, redistribution, derivative works, translation, commercial exploitation, AI model training or fine-tuning, AI-specific indexing, retrieval/grounding, generative reuse, synthetic narration, or synthetic translation;
- uses independently permitted by applicable law remain unaffected;
- hosting-provider terms remain separate and may grant the host rights under its agreement with the author.

A publication using this profile should carry all three of these together:

1. a visible copyright notice in the form `© YEAR COPYRIGHT OWNER. All Rights Reserved.`;
2. a human-readable publication-level `RIGHTS.md`;
3. a machine-readable `rights.json` conforming to `schemas/rights-v1.schema.json`.

For a published U.S. work, the notice year should be the year of first publication. Do not invent a Copyright Office registration number, effective date, ownership transfer, or publication date. Record those only from reliable source documents.
